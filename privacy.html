GFX Prod App — privacy

Short version: this app has no server. Nothing you type is sent anywhere.

What is stored, and where

Tasks, objectives, projects, the team roster, leave records, budgets and notes are held in your browser's own localStorage, under the key gfxprod.state.v1. That storage belongs to your browser profile on your device. It is not transmitted, backed up or synchronised by this application.

Microsoft 365

If — and only if — you enter an application (client) ID in Settings and sign in, the app talks directly from your browser to Microsoft Graph using delegated permissions. That means it can see exactly what you can see when you sign in to Microsoft 365, and nothing more. Access tokens are held by the Microsoft authentication library in your browser storage and are never sent to any third party. There is no back-end service, no service principal running in the background, and no password is ever seen or stored by this app.

Analytics

None. No telemetry, no cookies for tracking, no third-party analytics.

Third-party code

Two Microsoft-published scripts are loaded from Microsoft's CDNs when needed: the Teams JavaScript SDK and the MSAL browser authentication library. No other external code is used.

Deleting your data

Settings → Data → Clear all data, or clear site data for this origin in your browser. Either removes everything immediately and irreversibly.
