GFX Prod App — terms of use

This is an internal productivity tool, provided as-is, with no warranty of any kind.

It is not a system of record

Data lives in one browser profile. Clearing site data, changing machine or a reset profile takes it with it. Export a backup from Settings → Data regularly, and treat authoritative figures — payroll, approved budgets, official leave balances — as belonging to the systems that own them. This app models them; it does not replace them.

People data

If you record colleagues here, you are responsible for handling that information in line with your employer's policies and applicable data-protection law. Keep it to what you need to do your job, and do not publish it. The sample roster shipped with the app is fictional.

Microsoft 365 access

Any change you make to a SharePoint or OneDrive file through this app is a real change to that file, made as you, and recorded against your account. SharePoint version history and the recycle bin apply exactly as they would from the SharePoint web interface.
